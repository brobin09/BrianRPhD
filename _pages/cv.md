Education

* Ph.D. in History, University of South Carolina, Columbia, SC, 2018
* M.A. in History, North Carolina Central University, Durham, NC, 2011
* B.A. in History (Minor in Philosophy), North Carolina Central University, Durham, NC, 2009

Academic positions
======
* August 2022 – Present: Assistant Professor, Department of History
  * North Carolina Central University, Durham, NC
  * Instruction in African American history, U.S. history, and digital humanities; curriculum development integrating public history and digital scholarship; undergraduate and graduate mentoring. Chair, Student Success Committee.
* August 2017 – 2019: Instructor of Record, Department of History
  * North Carolina A&T State University, Greensboro, NC
  * Courses in African American and U.S. history emphasizing primary source analysis and critical thinking.
* 2019 – 2021: CLIR Postdoctoral Fellow in African American Studies
  * University of North Carolina at Greensboro
  * Project manager for *No Longer Yours: Slavery and Freedom in North Carolina*; contributor to the *People Not Property* slave deeds project.

Research and grants
======
* Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Equity Research Seed Grant, NCCU — Co-PI, *STEMulating the Humanities with COIL*, leveraging AI tools for Collaborative Online International Learning between the History Departments at NCCU and the University of Ghana.
* Faculty Research Awards Program, NCCU (Fall 2023) — Black history and Black cultural philosophy.
* Network to Freedom Grant, National Park Service — slavery and freedom seekers in North Carolina.
* Echoes of Black Durham, ACLS Digital Justice Grant (Fall 2025, pending) — advising on pedagogy, workflow design, and web host integration.

Book proposal
