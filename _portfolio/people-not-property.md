I contributed to the *People Not Property* slave deeds project, which digitizes and makes searchable bills of sale and deeds documenting enslaved people in North Carolina. The work is described in "People Not Property Project Overview" (with C. Heckel), published in the *Southern Quarterly*'s special double issue on the Digital South.

I continue to serve on the advisory board of the Digital Library on American Slavery at UNC-Greensboro.
