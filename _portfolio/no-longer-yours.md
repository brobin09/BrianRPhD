As a CLIR Postdoctoral Fellow at UNC-Greensboro (2019–2021), I served as project manager for *No Longer Yours: Slavery and Freedom in North Carolina*, developing digital humanities methodologies for making historical slavery records accessible to diverse audiences.

The accompanying Scalar publication, "Aspects of Slavery and Freedom Seeking in North Carolina," is available at [dlas.uncg.edu/interpretations/nly](https://dlas.uncg.edu/interpretations/nly/).
